Keeping Your Wauwatosa Water Heater Running Longer

Most early failures trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Drain and flush the tank every year to clear sediment, get the anode rod checked every few years, and test the relief valve each year so it doesn't seize. Keep the temperature near 120 degrees for safer water and less scale. If you have a tankless unit, descale it yearly where the water is hard to protect the heat exchanger.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models typically run 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing warning signs such as rusty hot water, rumbling during heating, moisture around the base, or showers that go cold sooner, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and far less stressful than an emergency one with water on the floor.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Take a spent anode rod: swapping it out is a short, affordable repair, but if nobody replaces it, the water begins corroding the tank itself, and just like that a minor fix has become a full replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a flooded utility room.

What Goes Wrong With Local Water Heaters

In Wauwatosa, we work on every kind of water heater, and each one has a typical way of failing. Gas tanks usually give trouble through pilot assemblies and thermocouples, dirty burners, and venting that doesn't draft. Electric tanks lose elements and thermostats, especially when sediment piles up around the lower element. Tankless heaters show error codes for scale buildup, ignition failures, and flow sensor trouble, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they don't do well in small, closed-in spaces. Our technicians work on all four every week and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
