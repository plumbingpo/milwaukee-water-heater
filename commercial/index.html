Kitchen Hot Water

Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest places to keep supplied with hot water. Every sink, sprayer, and dish cycle draws from the tank, a lunch rush can empty a small heater in minutes, and a high-temperature dish machine depends on a booster to reach its sanitizing temperature. Milwaukee Water Heater sizes, repairs, and maintains the tanks, tankless units, and booster heaters that keep both the kitchen and the restrooms supplied, and we plan visits around your hours.

Temperature is where many restaurant hot water problems start. If the main heater can't keep up, handwashing sinks run lukewarm, the three-compartment sink can't hold its wash temperature, and the dish machine struggles to sanitize during the rush. We verify temperatures from the tank all the way to the fixtures and document every visit in written reports your inspector and landlord can review.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits the hours you're open. When a job can't happen during service, we do it after close, so hot water is ready when the first shift arrives.

Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can result in carbon monoxide hazards from bad venting, scalding at the tap, bacterial growth in tanks kept too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Milwaukee Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas piping sizing, relief valve discharge piping, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where local code calls for it, and permits and inspections. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Regular professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and following through on our recommendations helps keep small issues from becoming emergencies. It also creates a record. We keep service logs, temperature records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and inspectors that you've done your due diligence.

Rapid Response

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and spark tenant disputes. Milwaukee Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties across Milwaukee and surrounding areas, and we schedule non-urgent work after hours or before opening so your doors stay open.

Our technicians are equipped to handle the full range of commercial water heater emergencies, including gas valve failures, burned-out elements, ignition and flame sensor faults, high-limit lockouts, leaking tanks and fittings, failed recirculation pumps, and tankless units stuck in lockout. We prioritize diagnosing the fault and restoring hot water as quickly as possible, valving off a failed unit so the rest of the system keeps running, then put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again. Every commercial response includes thorough documentation for your service records.

Ongoing Protection

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

Our commercial maintenance program gets built around your equipment, how hard the system works, and your budget. On each scheduled visit, we handle anode checks and tank flushing, descaling of tankless heat exchangers, relief valve testing, burner, flue, and gas valve inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve service, and temperature readings at key fixtures. Mineral-heavy water shortens the life of commercial equipment quickly, so busy properties usually need more frequent visits.
